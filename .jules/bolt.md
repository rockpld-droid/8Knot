## 2024-10-25 - Pandas row-by-row apply bottleneck
**Learning:** In the `8Knot` codebase, visualizations calculated open issues and pull requests by iterating over each day in a date range using `df.apply(..., axis=1)`, creating an $O(N \times D)$ bottleneck. Vectorizing this using `np.searchsorted` brought down the calculation time from multiple seconds to milliseconds (~500x speedup). The logic for `pr_review_response.py` had a sliding window, making full vectorization complicated, so it was reverted to maintain functionality, serving as a reminder that complex rolling window conditions may not be trivially vectorizable with standard `np.searchsorted`.
**Action:** Next time, look for `.apply(..., axis=1)` calls involving dataframes and dates. If the logic is finding events (like creation and closure) before/after a threshold, `np.searchsorted` or cumulative sums of event arrays can often vectorize the process for massive speedups. However, be cautious with sliding-window conditions which may require more complex segment tree or explicit loop logic to maintain correctness.
